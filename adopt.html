♥ How Adoption Works ♥

At Paws Cat Rescue, we want every cat to find a safe and loving home. Our adoption process helps us make sure each cat is matched with the right family.

Adoption Process

If you are looking to adopt a cat and welcome a new family member into your life, we want to make sure you are prepared. Below is our adoption process to help ensure our cats find safe and loving homes.

  1. Check our social media pages or visit our shelter to meet and connect with our furry friends.
  2. Complete an adoption application by contacting us or filling out the adoption form on our Contact Us page. After submitting your information, we will send the full application to your email.
  3. Meet with our team and learn more about your chosen cat.
  4. Bring your new furry friend home! ♥

Adoption Information

All adoption requirements and important information will be provided in the adoption application. Please review our short application carefully to make sure you are prepared to welcome a new furry friend home. ♥
