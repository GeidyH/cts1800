♥ Join Our Volunteer Team ♥

At Paws Cat Rescue, our volunteers play an important role in helping cats receive the care and attention they need. By volunteering your time, you can help make a difference in the lives of cats waiting to find their forever family.

How You Can Help

  • Help care for cats by providing food and comfort.
  • Spend time socializing with cats to help them feel safe and loved.
  • Assist during adoption events and community activities.
  • Spread awareness about cat adoption and rescue.

Become a Volunteer

If you would like to join our volunteer team, please contact us through our Contact Us page. We would love to have caring people help us give cats the support and love they deserve. ♥
