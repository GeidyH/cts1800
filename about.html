♥ About Paws Cat Rescue ♥

Paws Cat Rescue is dedicated to rescuing cats and kittens in need and helping them find safe and loving homes. Our goal is to provide care, comfort, and support for cats

We believe every cat deserves a second chance. Our rescue helps provide food, shelter, medical care, and attention to cats who have been abandoned, lost, or are looking for a new home.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the lives of cats by connecting them with responsible and caring adopters while providing resources and guidance to families throughout the adoption process.

What We DO

  • Rescue cats and kittens in need
  • Provide food, shelter and medical care
  • Find cats their loving family
  • Support responsible pet ownership
